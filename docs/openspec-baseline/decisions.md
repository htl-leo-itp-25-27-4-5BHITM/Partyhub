# Decisions and unresolved questions

Foundation snapshot: `9487ccb90bb438e24b3cfab547a5dc900b11aecb`, 2026-09-21; Step 2 integration on 2026-09-23 and Steps 3-4 integration on 2026-09-25. D001-D016 are accepted. Group 4's lifecycle/validation/client contract is integrated through `document-party-lifecycle`. Full requirement and scenario links are in [coverage.md](coverage.md).

An observed implementation is not an approved policy. An open Q item is assigned for a later domain review, not a blocker to completing the inventory stage. A downstream requirement depending on that question cannot be silently accepted until its answer is supported by existing decisions or a new explicit decision.
